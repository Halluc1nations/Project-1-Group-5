# Group Cost Calculator

A simple, intuitive tool to help groups fairly split shared expenses. Enter each member’s name and their individual expenses, and the calculator will evenly distribute the total cost so everyone pays their fair share.

---

## 🔥 Features

- **Add Multiple Members:** Enter as many group members as needed.
- **Record Expenses:** Input each person’s individual expenses.
- **Automatic Calculations:** Computes the total group cost and each person’s share.
- **Clean Interface:** Easy‑to‑use design for quick expense management.

---

## 🚀 Quick Start

1. **Add Group Members**  
   - Enter the names of everyone in your group.  
   - Example: `Alice`, `Bob`, `Charlie`.

2. **Enter Expenses**  
   - For each member, add the dollar amount they spent.  
   - Example:  
     - Alice: `$50`  
     - Bob: `$75`  
     - Charlie: `$25`

3. **Calculate Final Split**  
   - Click the **Calculate** button (or “Cancel,” depending on UI) to process all entries.  
   - The tool sums all expenses and divides by the number of members.

4. **View Results**  
   - **Total Spent:** The combined group expense.  
   - **Per‑Person Share:** How much each member should pay.  
   - **Settlement Amounts:** Who owes whom and how much to balance out expenses.

---

## 📊 Example

> **Members:** Alice, Bob, Charlie  
> **Expenses:**  
> - Alice: `$50`  
> - Bob: `$75`  
> - Charlie: `$25`  
>
> **Result:**  
> - **Total Expenses:** `$150`  
> - **Each Share:** `$50`  
> - **Settlement:**  
>   - Bob paid `$25` more than their share, so Bob receives `$25` back.  
>   - Charlie paid `$25` less, so Charlie owes `$25`.  
>   - Alice paid exactly her share.

---

## 💡 Notes & Best Practices

- Double‑check all expense entries to ensure accurate results.
- The tool assumes an **equal split** among participants.  
- If someone needs to be excluded from a particular expense, enter their expense as `$0` or remove them from the calculation temporarily.
